Privacy Policy for Screenful

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data collection (none)

We do not collect, store, transmit or share your recordings, personal data or device information. Screenful has no servers and no accounts.

2. Local storage only

Your screen recordings, their still pictures and your settings are stored only on your own device: the video files and pictures in the app's private storage, and the rest in a local SQLite database. No other app can read them. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so nothing is copied to Google's cloud or to a new phone unless you choose a folder yourself (see 6).

3. Screen capture, the microphone and notifications

Screenful records the screen only after you tap Start and approve Android's own "start recording" prompt, and a notification and the system recording indicator show for as long as it runs. The microphone is used only if you turn on "Record the microphone"; the sound the phone plays is recorded only if you turn on "Record phone sound". Screenful records what is on the screen, which can include other apps and notifications: you are responsible for what you record. Screens that apps protect (for example banking apps) appear black, as Android intends. If you turn on the floating button, Screenful draws over other apps (Android's "Display over other apps"), and "Show taps" uses Android's "Modify system settings"; neither is used for anything else.

4. No internet access

Screenful asks for no internet permission. The only network connection involved is Google Play's own, used for the optional Pro subscription. Recordings never leave your phone unless you share one.

5. Speech to text

If you ask, or turn on "Turn speech into text by itself", Screenful writes down what is said in a recording. This is done by a speech model inside the app, on your phone's own processor. The sound and the words never leave your phone, and no speech service is used. The text is stored on your device with the recording and is removed with it.

6. Copying to a folder

If you choose a folder in Share to a folder, Screenful copies your recordings and a list of their details (titles, folders, tags, chapters and, if there are any, the words) into it. The folder can be inside a cloud app such as Google Drive or Dropbox; that app, not Screenful, uploads it, and its own privacy policy applies. If you turn on encryption, every copied file is locked with a passphrase that only you know. Screenful does not keep the passphrase and cannot recover it; if you forget it the copies cannot be opened.

7. Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's share sheet sends the video to the app you choose. From that moment that app's own privacy policy applies. If you record other people, please respect their privacy and the laws where you live.

8. The app lock

The optional lock uses Android's own fingerprint, face or PIN prompt. Screenful never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

9. Deleting your data

Settings, Clear all data removes every recording and setting. Uninstalling the app removes everything too.

10. Children

Screenful collects nothing from anyone, including children.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
